# Offline qualification evidence assessment

`qualification.Check(io.Reader)` parses an at-most 1 MiB strict JSON record. It rejects unknown, missing, case-aliased and duplicate keys recursively. It returns an HW-01 through HW-14 matrix, blockers and a reproducible identity fingerprint. The fingerprint binds drive, firmware, host, utility, deployment and record/flush/position settings. Cartridge labels and blank provenance remain recorded separately from the drive fingerprint.

The checked-in record template stays explicitly synthetic and pending. Every required physical test needs a PASS outcome, supervised observation time, required assertions, relative output-evidence references with SHA-256, literal observed commands and exit codes. Raw-byte comparison evidence needs matching counts and hashes. HW-05 accounts for both data and commit files from two appends. HW-12 needs three baseline and three application trials over the same dataset, matching record/compression settings, separate readback measurements, CPU/RSS observations and the roadmap's 80 percent target. The configured maximum record bound is the qualified reader limit, capped at 1 MiB, while raw drive-limit output belongs in HW-01 evidence.

A complete self-reported physical record becomes `eligible_for_operator_review`. Reports always leave `hardware_qualified` and `write_approval` false. The validator reads no referenced files, verifies no operator identity, opens no devices and executes no command. It cannot create a profile, permit, initialization arm or enrollment. An operator must review authentic logs and the actual host independently. Simulator, synthetic, mixed, missing, failed, partial and skipped evidence withhold review eligibility.

Complete independent recovery with a second operator and the application/catalog unavailable. Capture all six comparable performance trials, end-of-media/interruption evidence, soak and deployment parity. Preserve failed/skipped rows and limitations.

Use `tapepilot qualification-check FILE` to inspect the offline record. The assessment may identify missing evidence and fingerprint changes. Exact device/driver command arguments must come from the authorized host's installed utilities, confirmed nonrewinding node and observed profile. The candidate worksheet and ROADMAP.md retain the authoritative sequence and stop conditions.
